## What Was Fixed

1. **Updated to Secure Version**: Changed from 4.22.1 to **4.25.1-lts** (latest secure version)
2. **Image Upload Enabled**: Added proper image upload configuration
3. **Upload Directory Created**: Created `media/ckeditor_uploads/` directory

## How to Use Image Upload

1. **In CKEditor Content Area**:
   - Click on the **Image** button in the toolbar
   - Click "Browse Server" or "Upload" tab
   - Upload your image
   - Image will be inserted into content

2. **Alternative Method**:
   - You can also paste image URLs directly
   - Or use drag & drop if supported
